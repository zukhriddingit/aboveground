## Interpretation

The plaza is a historical composite. The four supplied reference photographs are used for observation only. All visible surfaces and lettering are drawn by the kit. Nineteen of 34 building records lack both height and levels. Elevations and photographs guide proportions, colours, and roof pitches. Dormer and mullion spacing, the gate ironwork, the exact grade and seating of the Pit, the busker’s instrument and case, participants, shop displays, canopy mix, grave marker placement, weather and trips are illustrative. Tree positions, the burial-ground boundary, commercial envelopes and the directions of the three station flights follow OSM. The Eliot Square entrance is outside this plate.
