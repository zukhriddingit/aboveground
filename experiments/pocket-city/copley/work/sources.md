# Copley Square field sources

- The supplied extract gives the building and roof polygons, right-hand street layout, 66 mapped tree positions, crossings and business names. The prepared scene also places two illustrative park trees. [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=18/42.350065/-71.078142)
- The finish milestone on Boylston is at 42.3497514, −71.0786162. Applying the supplied plot transform gives (−48.51, −19.26) m, 71.49 m inside the nearest edge. [OpenStreetMap · finish marker](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/7644779165)
- McKim’s perimeter wraps an open court. Its bearing walls have Milford granite facing. The drawings distinguish McKim from the attached Johnson building. [Boston Public Library](https://www.bpl.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/30/2022/03/Volume-1_Executive-Summary.pdf)
- McKim plan, Dartmouth elevation, triple-arched entrance and courtyard arcade. The local historical front elevation was also used to count the main window bays. [Boston Landmarks Commission](https://www.bostonpreservation.org/sites/default/files/2024-05/boston_public_library_central_branch_99.pdf)
- Cummings & Sears gave Old South a campanile and a copper lantern. [Old South Church](https://oldsouth.org/history)
- Exterior view of the campanile, copper crossing lantern, contrasting masonry and entry portico. [Old South exterior photograph](https://www.oldsouth.org/news/new-chapter-racial-justice-work)
- Boston guide pp. 17–19: cruciform plan, Venetian Gothic, Roxbury puddingstone, contrasting limestone and brownstone, photographs and 230-foot rebuilt tower. [Sacred Space International](https://www.pbs.org/godinamerica/art/bos_cityguide2.pdf)
- The printed Old South sanctuary floor diagram provides a second check on the plan. The mapped roof parts locate the crossing and the corner campanile separately. [Metropolitan Wind Symphony](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/611159e445fb3406c889da10/t/65e2344a0c9ca25bd2102f3b/1709323338823/MWC_Program_20240303.pdf)
- The 1914 headhouse by the library was preserved in the Copley station renovation. Figure 3-2 shows its ornamental iron façade. [Federal Transit Administration](https://www.transit.dot.gov/sites/fta.dot.gov/files/docs/regulations-and-guidance/environmental-programs/63526/ftahistoriccontextreport508compliant.pdf)
- Corner row house, archival and contemporary views, commercial remodelling and projecting bays. Bay and stoop details on neighbouring narrow lots are illustrative. [Back Bay Houses · 277 Dartmouth](https://backbayhouses.org/277-dartmouth-147-newbury/)
- Exeter’s former auditorium has a Romanesque stone exterior, Newbury porch and gabled roof. The historical account and photographs distinguish its tall gables from neighbouring row houses. [Back Bay Houses · 26 Exeter](https://backbayhouses.org/26%20Exeter-181-Newbury/)
- The renewed square includes an event plaza on Dartmouth and a planted grove. Only the western part of the square falls within this plot. [Sasaki](https://www.sasaki.com/projects/copley-square-park/)
- Construction photographs document the plaza pavers and their mixed warm tones. [Boston Parks and Recreation](https://www.boston.gov/sites/default/files/file/2024/11/Copley%20Square%20Park%20-%20November%202024%20Update.docx.pdf)
- The Copley BosTix booth reopened in 2025. The front-page photograph informs the kiosk and poster panels. [The Boston Sun](https://thebostonsun.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/TBS0806.pdf)
- 1991–2020 Logan normals give a mean daily high of 56.4°F in April and 82.1°F in July, and mean January snowfall of 14.3 inches. [NOAA · Boston Logan normals](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&endDate=9996-12-31&format=pdf&startDate=0001-01-01&stations=USW00014739)
- The Arboretum documents maple fall colour in Boston. This is a regional colour reference, not a species inventory at Copley. [Arnold Arboretum of Harvard University](https://arboretum.harvard.edu/plants/maple-collection-directions-and-accessibility-information/)
- The B.A.A. finish-area diagram puts the finish chute on Boylston before Dartmouth, with the runners travelling toward Dartmouth. [Boston Athletic Association](https://www.baa.org/wp-content/uploads/drupal-legacy/2023-04/Pocket%20Guide%20Finish%20Area.pdf)
- The painting crew describes maintaining the blue and yellow Boylston finish marking throughout the year. [RoadSafe Traffic Systems](https://www.roadsafetraffic.com/roadsafe-paints-boston-marathon-start-and-finish-lines/)
- The Copley Square farmers market runs on Tuesdays and Fridays during its season. [Mass Farmers Markets](https://www.massfarmersmarkets.org/news/wpjyswe7yk299w88gymdn5y8y3pg4w)
