# Hynes station field sources

## Geometry and placement

The 236 m square is turned 26° counterclockwise from east in the original east–north plane. Its centre is 42.3480307, −71.0879450. The Mass Ave door is at plot (0.00, −19.70) m. This puts the centre between the threshold and the Newbury corner. The original placement was 124.5 m from its requested point. The final placement uses a pinned centre and zero slide.

The plot-only copy gives distinct display names to the underground station relation (20270834) and the exhibition hall (18889572). They share one OSM name. Station and hall polygons were tested independently. The hall, its Boylston façade and the Berklee Performance Center all lie outside. A temporary copy of the supplied extract marks the Turnpike below street level for prep.py. The open highway sections, Tommy Leonard Bridge on Massachusetts Avenue and O’Leary Bridge on Boylston use their surveyed geometry. The station’s separate pedestrian bridge follows its mapped approach, which continues off the plate. [Mass Ave bridge](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/818120363) · [Boylston road bridge](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/826552816) · [Station approach](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/802339166). The station’s underground outline locates its surface rooms.

Run `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 ~/.venvs/pocket-city/bin/python work/prepare.py` from the Hynes experiment to repeat plot.py and prep.py. The final pass assigns surveyed addresses, separates the exposed Lyrik deck and clips surface routes to the slab, building walls and highway openings. Storefronts by address, the cut grade of the Pike and the two photographed headhouses are drawn at the surface.

## Interpretation

The plate depicts an illustrative Boston day using the cached street geometry and exterior references from several dates. It makes no service or event-calendar claims. Original photographs are credited as drawing references and are not embedded. Sixteen ordinary footprints meet the boundary. The rail and road grades, roof plant, unmeasured heights, trees beyond the sixteen mapped positions, bay spacing, facade colours and animation timings are approximate. No underground passenger facilities are shown.


## Validation

The final plot contains 58 mapped building footprints. Sixteen ordinary footprints meet the edge and all seven entries in the placement report are whole. Forty-six footprints lack a mapped height or storey count. The scene retains sixteen mapped trees and adds eleven illustrative street trees plus the hand-drawn Lyrik terrace planting.

The embedded renderer completed all 60 combinations of the six states and ten preset cameras. Visual checks also covered six overview rotations, the high view, four-times zoom, each hand-built landmark and a 420 × 680 phone canvas. A separate pass sampled 7,099 moving pedestrian positions and checked the 219 surface route segments against the slab, building walls and open highway cuts. The Open Newbury scene removes three car-route segments and two bike-route segments from the pedestrianised block while Mass Ave traffic continues. Both occasions produced their own props and crowds. Water, boats and night lighting do not apply to these scenes.

Chrome could not start its headless process in the sandbox. The checks loaded the completed HTML from disk and ran its own frame renderer with a local Canvas implementation. The visual review and geometry checks passed. Native browser controls and browser frame rate were not measured. The HTML contains its scene data, scripts and artwork inline. The local visual record is [the preview sheet](../shots/hynes-preview.jpg).
