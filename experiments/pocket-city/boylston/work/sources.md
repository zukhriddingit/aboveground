# Boylston station and the Boston Common edge

## Plot and map

The supplied 220 × 220 m square is centered at 42.3533577° N, 71.0647674° W and turned −4°. The two headhouses, the Parkman Bandstand, the Grand Lodge and The Parkside all fit. Along the western edge the square includes about 3,930 m² of the Central Burying Ground. The whole square contains 19 building footprints, 81 mapped trees, two illustrative park trees, 35 green areas, nine marked crossings and eight named points of interest. Six ordinary buildings continue beyond the edge. [OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=18/42.35336/-71.06477) · [Mapped burial-ground enclosure](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/29818933)

## The granite headhouses

Boylston and Park Street opened on September 1, 1897, on the first subway in the United States. The Boston Transit Commission’s headhouse elevation and subsequent station plan establish the two elongated enclosures. The two mapped buildings are about 11 m long and 5.7 m wide, with their entrances about 82 m apart. The modern doorway treatments come from the four photographs below. [Boston Society of Civil Engineers Section, history of the Tremont Street Subway](https://www.bscesjournal.org/wp-content/uploads/CEP-Vol-13-No-2-03.pdf) · [National Historic Landmark nomination](https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NHLS/66000788_text) · [1897 commission plate 10](https://wardmaps.com/products/btc-report-03-1897-plate-10-boylston-street-station-head-houses-boston-massachusetts) · [1898 station plan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boylston_plan.png)

## Buildings and park

- **Parkman Bandstand.** The City’s existing-conditions drawing locates the circular platform, northern access and radial brick walks. The contemporary and archival photographs establish the raised base, eight slender columns, railings and shallow domed roof. The pavilion was dedicated in 1912. Its six-metre-scale height and individual molding dimensions are estimated. The plotted roof and the northern stairs fit within the square. [Boston Common Master Plan, Bandstand section](https://www.boston.gov/sites/default/files/file/2023/05/bmcp-full-report.pdf) · [Friends of the Public Garden](https://friendsofthepublicgarden.org/our-parks/the-common/) · [Contemporary photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Parkman_Bandstand.JPG) · [Boston Public Library archival photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boston,_Massachusetts,_Boston_Common,_Parkman_Memorial_Bandstand_-_DPLA_-_c27ce4ecb06e6b50491cc2d54be51811.jpg)
- **Masonic Grand Lodge.** National Register inventory entry TH-18 records nine stories, granite, architects Loring & Phipps and an 1899 date. The Lodge’s own account gives 1898 as the building date and identifies the Boylston Street public entrance. The Sanborn plate locates the footprint at the corner. The wide window arches, rustication, chamfer and cornice follow the exterior photographs. The blue-and-gold Tremont mosaics interpret the 2010 photograph. The mapped main roof height is 38.5 m. [National Register inventory](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/1ccc770c-b917-4b4b-8cc4-dde9a8a89c27) · [Grand Lodge visitor guide](https://massfreemasonry.org/boston-masonic-building/) · [1909 Sanborn plan, plate 42](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sanborn_Fire_Insurance_Map_from_Boston,_Suffolk_County,_Massachusetts,_1909,_Volume_1,_Plate_0042.jpg) · [Street-corner photographs](https://lostnewengland.com/2014/09/masonic-temple-boston/) · [Jyothis, Tremont mosaics](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grand_Lodge_of_Masons_in_Boston.JPG)
- **The Parkside and Tremont storefronts.** The Parkside’s mapped footprint, 18 levels and 51 m height anchor the custom brick front. Exterior photographs informed the central canopy, pale trim and repeated residential bays. The shop names come from the supplied map. The inbound and outbound street photographs locate the glazed cinema frontage and vertical theatre sign in relation to the subway. The Coard and Ansin buildings use the district inventory’s materials and massing. [Parkside plan outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/29650697) · [The Parkside, exterior photographs](https://www.backbayresidential.com/buildings/parkside-condominiums-170-tremont-street/) · [Warren Residential, 170 Tremont](https://warrenre.com/developments/the-parkside) · [National Register entries TH-16 and TH-17](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/1ccc770c-b917-4b4b-8cc4-dde9a8a89c27)

## Rebuilding

From the repository root, use the supplied Pocket City environment. The first command regenerates the surface scene from the cached extract. The second writes the standalone page using only that scene and the two authored source files.

```sh
~/.venvs/pocket-city/bin/python experiments/pocket-city/boylston/work/prepare.py
~/.venvs/pocket-city/bin/python ~/.codex/skills/pocket-city/scripts/build.py \
  --scene experiments/pocket-city/boylston/work/scene.json \
  --city experiments/pocket-city/boylston/work/city.json \
  --landmarks experiments/pocket-city/boylston/work/landmarks.js \
  --out frontend/public/pocket/boylston.html
```
