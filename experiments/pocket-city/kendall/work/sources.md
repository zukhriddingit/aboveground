# Kendall/MIT · field sources

## Plot and map

- The 246 × 246 m plate is centred at 42.3625553° N, 71.0865922° W and turned −6° to follow Main Street. Increasing the supplied square by 16 m and moving its centre 6.1 m keeps the entire Marriott footprint behind Kendall Plaza. It also keeps 314 Main, both station entrances, Engine 7, 325 Main and the E37/E38 group. The southern edge crosses part of Kendall/MIT Open Space. [OpenStreetMap contributors · block](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=19/42.36255/-71.08659)
- The supplied OSM extract is dated 2026-11-22 UTC. Preparation produced 46 building, part or roof records, 55 mapped broadleaf trees, nine green areas, seven crossings, 19 named places and 89 route records. Nine ordinary building parts meet a plot edge. All five station features and both hotels in the plotter's keep report are whole. Seven station or roof records have neither a positive storey count nor a height. [OpenStreetMap contributors · ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
- The two Red Line tracks are mapped underground. The west edge intersects their centrelines at plot y = −12.59 and −8.93 m, the east edge at −12.15 and −8.35 m. The two end sections use those positions. Their vertical dimensions are compressed to fit the kit's slab. [Track 2](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/340542803) · [Track 1](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/361558867)
- Matisse installed the three-part Kendall Band in the station. Pythagoras hangs between the tracks. The Pythagoras POI belongs underground. [Paul Matisse · Kendall Band](https://www.paulmatisse.com/kendall-band)

## Architecture and street level

- NADAAA's gateway plan separates the stair, escalator and lift. The shell tapers in opposite directions at its two ends. Its underside is flat, its concealed upper surface pitched, and the transverse soffit strips incorporate lighting. The columns have irregular spacing. [NADAAA · Kendall/MIT Gateway](https://www.nadaaa.com/portfolio/kendall-mit-gateway/)
- The inbound station opened in February 2023. Northstar gives a length of 160 ft and 24 stainless steel supports about 26 ft high. The model takes its length from the surveyed roof, uses 24 supports and pitches the upper faces to the two gutters. Individual column locations are approximate. [Northstar · completed station](https://northstar-pres.com/kendall-mit-mbta-station-opens-to-the-public/)
- The canopy polygon and the three kiosk footprints come from different OSM features. In two relations the outer perimeter consists of multiple open ways. Joining the outer endpoints recovers each complete entrance outline. [Canopy](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/20779868) · [North stair](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/20779871) · [Elevator](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/20779869) · [South entry](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1307837143)
- The outbound street-level envelope and lift occupy nested mapped footprints on the north side of Main. [Outbound hall](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1524505563) · [Elevator](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1524505564)
- The rebuilt outbound headhouse opened on April 20, 2026. It has street-level glazing, a second elevator and a public rooftop patio connected to the UP Porch. The finished photo below governs the elevation and fascia. [Cambridge Redevelopment Authority · opening and render](https://www.cambridgeredevelopment.org/in-the-news/kendall-headhouse-is-open-press-release) · [CRA · photo gallery](https://www.cambridgeredevelopment.org/projects/kendall-headhouse)
- The landscape design submission shows the plaza between Main Street and the Marriott, its lawn, pavement, movable chairs and connection to the upper porch. See printed sheets 69 and 71 onward for photos, plans and sections. [City of Cambridge · 325 Main design review](https://www.cambridgema.gov/-/media/Files/CDD/ZoningDevel/specialpermitsadvisory/sp315/sp315_dr_bldgb_ch24_20180906.pdf)
- **314 Main.** WEISS/MANFREDI's 17-storey building carries a faceted office tower over a four-storey base. Terracotta-coloured panels establish the lower register and the glazing becomes more transparent towards the top. [WEISS/MANFREDI · Site 5](https://www.weissmanfredi.com/projects/2140-multi-use-building-for-mit-s-kendall-square-initiative)
- Site 5's ground plan, north and east elevations, inset fourth-floor terrace, curtain-wall reveals and roof screen informed the three volumes. See printed sheets 38–41. The outline changes at the mapped heights 16.5, 21.0 and 85.4 m. [City of Cambridge · Building 5 architectural submission](https://www.cambridgema.gov/-/media/files/cdd/zoningdevel/specialpermitsadvisory/sp302_303_mit/sp303_bldg5_design_submission.pdf) · [Gambrill Center outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1254628669)
- The ground-floor plan puts the bookstore entrance on Main and the museum entrance on the east plaza. The museum's own photographs show its glazed entry and lettering. The small books and display objects in the model are illustrative. [Graffito · 314 Main plan](https://graffito.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/MITIMCO-Properties-Kendall-040323.pdf) · [MIT Museum · FY25 illustrated report](https://mit-museum-cms-files--production.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/MITMus_Ann_Rprt_FY25-FINAL.pdf?v=1761929459)
- **Engine 7.** The hotel retained the firehouse, restored two cupolas and built a seven-storey addition behind it. The hotel gallery and Kalwall's oblique roof views show the brick and arched windows, front sign, dormers and darker hipped roof. The site plan in the Building 5 submission places the two masses on opposite ends of the lot. [The Kendall Hotel · Engine 7 history](https://www.kendallhotel.com/about-us/engine-7/) · [Hotel gallery](https://www.kendallhotel.com/gallery/) · [Kalwall · exterior and aerial views](https://www.kalwall.com/project/kendall-hotel-cambridge-ma/)
- **325 Main.** The office building shifts in plan and has inset terraces. The built photographs show a dark-framed curtain wall and the street/park connection. The exterior stair, terrace and bridge meet the outbound hall on its west side. [Pickard Chilton · Google Cambridge](https://www.pickardchilton.com/work/google-cambridge) · [Stantec · 325 Main Street](https://www.stantec.com/en/projects/united-states-projects/-/325-mail-street)
- **Marriott.** The City design submission locates the setback Marriott tower behind the north plaza. The OSM tower part has a stepped back and a straight face towards the station. The red-brown wall, vertical window strips and white sign also appear in the supplied station photographs. The model removes the garage and west wing from the broad podium outline and uses the tower footprint for the tall mass. [Tower footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/824367467) · [Overall footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/132600546) · [City of Cambridge · surrounding context](https://www.cambridgema.gov/-/media/files/cdd/zoningdevel/specialpermitsadvisory/sp302_303_mit/sp303_bldg5_design_submission.pdf)
- **MIT Site 4.** The cantilevered Graduate Tower uses the shifted Café Wall panel pattern. The adjacent six-level concrete warehouse houses InnovationHQ. The mapped base, tower, brick shells and Welcome Center frame the east side of the inbound canopy. Panel widths, colours and the storey-to-storey pattern are simplified. [NADAAA · Site 4](https://www.nadaaa.com/portfolio/mit-mixed-use-development/) · [MIT Capital Projects · Site 4](https://capitalprojects.mit.edu/projects/kendall-square-site-4)
- In fall 2024 Cambridge installed quick-build separated bike lanes from Ames Street to Galaxy Park. Fine white buffers and bicycle markings follow Main Street in both directions. The marking intervals, sky reflections, tables, lamps and vehicle mix are illustrative. [City of Cambridge · Cycling Safety Ordinance, printed p. 10](https://www.cambridgema.gov/-/media/Files/Traffic/cyclingsafetyordinance/csoyear5report.pdf)

## Rendering checks

The skill build validated the season and occasion sources, month mapping and cameras. Chrome's headless debugging endpoint did not start in the sandbox. The fallback loaded the drawing blocks from the built HTML on disk and ran its own canvas renderer in the page's colour-wash finish. Checks included six whole-plot yaws, a bird's-eye, named cameras, close-ups, both rendering profiles, all six season/occasion choices, and six sides plus a roof view of each hand-built building group. The passenger routes were sampled through 150 seconds in each scene against the ground-floor building rings. Geometry, views and field notes are bundled in the page. The kit's controls were not exercised in Chrome.
