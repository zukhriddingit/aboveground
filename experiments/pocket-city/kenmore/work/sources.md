# Kenmore Square field sources

## The plate

- The plate is 240 × 240 m, centered at 42.3489922° N, 71.0949241° W. Its x-axis is turned 6.17° north of east. It contains the convergence of Beacon Street and Commonwealth Avenue, the bus island, hotel, 660 Beacon, 610 Beacon and the Commonwealth Avenue Mall. Footprints, way directions, tagged storeys, crossings, tree positions and business names come from the supplied ODbL extract. [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=19/42.348992/-71.094924)
- The CITGO attraction is a *node*. Its host is the unnamed office way with address 660 Beacon. A name filter on buildings alone misses it. Containment was checked against the complete pre-clip polygons. The old sign node is 14.02 m inside. The host and rear hotel wing each clear the boundary by about 0.06 m in this extract. The main hotel clears it by 1.99 m and 610 Beacon by 14.14 m. These are computational clearances, not cadastral accuracy. [Sign node](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/7599402961) · [660 Beacon footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/29559484) · [Main hotel](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/29650741) · [Rear hotel](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/93458629)
- Immediately west, the WHOOP building is wholly beyond the plate. The nearest part of the mapped Fenway stadium outline lies about 77 m beyond the southern plot edge. The game-day walk continues off the plate. [WHOOP footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1188211556) · [Fenway stadium relation](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/18354422)
- The kit prepared 75 footprints or parts, including 21 ordinary edge cuts. Sixty have neither a mapped height nor a storey count. There are 24 mapped trees, 15 crossing markings, 18 named places or shops, and no water or boats. The mapped Kenmore Row common is drawn as paved frontage, as seen in the south-entrance photograph. [Mapped frontage](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/832992978) · [South entrance photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:South_entrance_to_Kenmore_station,_July_2019.JPG)

## Buildings and the bus island

- The Boston Landmarks Commission's 2018 study contains the parcel maps, aerial view, site photographs and physical description of the historic sign. Section 2.2 describes two 60-foot square faces on steel trusses, the lower edge roughly 40 feet above the six-storey roof, a raised three-facet red triangle, blue lettering, and white enameled steel. The model uses these dimensions, the old OSM point and its opposing 65°/245° bearings. The host roof is an estimate from six mapped storeys. The historical truss-to-roof proportions take precedence over the separate point's 43 m height tag. [Boston Landmarks Commission study](https://www.boston.gov/sites/default/files/imce-uploads/2018-09/citgo_sign_subcommittee_final_draft.pdf) · [OSM sign and bearings](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/7599402961)
- A Massachusetts Historical Commission building inventory reproduced in the Kenmore Square North filing records the sign on its truss at 660 Beacon. The parcel and photo plates are additional references for its commercial setting. [Kenmore Square North filing, including survey photographs](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-10/kenmore-sq-north-beacon-bldg-boston-ma-2019.pdf)
- CITGO documented the completed repositioning and public relighting in November 2026. The plate intentionally retains the earlier rooftop siting shown in the supplied map and the 2018 city study. [CITGO's November 20 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bostons-iconic-sign-shines-again-over-kenmore-square-302947198.html)
- Simpson Gumpertz & Heger dates the canopy project to 2008 and describes a conical section, approximately 26 by 130 feet, with nine stainless-steel trusses on 16-foot centers and structural glass. The tapered map way is the outer envelope. The structural rhythm follows the engineer, with the curve and glass divisions fitted inside the OSM outline. [Structural engineer, description and project gallery](https://www.sgh.com/project/mbta-kenmore-square-bus-station-canopy/) · [Mapped canopy](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/308329579)
- The station architect illustrates the conical scheme and paired arc trusses, shows the old and finished shelters, and describes access from the busway and from the hotel. Turn-of-the-century-style streetlamps informed the lantern models at mapped lamp points. [DiMella Shaffer design account and drawings](https://www.dimellashaffer.com/blog/seeing-is-believing/) · [Architect's gallery](https://www.dimellashaffer.com/projects/kenmore-square-t-station/)
- The MBTA's photograph of bus 19 and the Boston Planning Department's photograph of buses in the island informed the windshield, dark window band, yellow stripe and position of the boarding bus. The bus is illustrative. [MBTA bus guide](https://www.mbta.com/guides/bus-guide) · [Fenway Transportation Action Plan](https://www.bostonplans.org/planning-zoning/planning-initiatives/fenway-transportation-action-plan)
- The supplied station survey records surface thresholds. North sidewalk steps follow way 1469613370, island steps follow ways 731987527, 731987529 and 835548597, and the south stair opens into the hotel's front façade. There are two distinct surface elevators and a small busway information booth. [North stair](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1469613370) · [Hotel stair threshold](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/7776200202) · [Hotel lift](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/832992981) · [Island lift](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/835548607) · [Information booth](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/835548605)
- Schopfer Associates' design and street views show the hotel's retail base, integrated station opening and avenue frontage. The Society of Architectural Historians identifies the mansard and false dormers. The model follows the 20.6 m mapped envelope and draws the pale projecting bays, storefront arches, dormers and dark entrance canopy. [Original architect and gallery](https://www.schopferassociates.com/hospitality-hotel-commonwealth) · [SAH architectural survey](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/MA-01-WB1) · [Hotel gallery](https://www.hotelcommonwealth.com/gallery/)
- The expansion faces the Turnpike, with parking, three guestroom levels and enclosed connections back to the old hotel. The city reviewed a seven-storey addition approximately 65 feet high. The rendered addition uses that overall height because the OSM parking outline records only four levels. The curtain wall and garage screen follow the expansion team's exterior photographs. [Boston Redevelopment Authority board filing](https://www.bostonplans.org/getattachment/6e099e33-0f28-462c-ae85-daf0759278fc) · [Columbia Construction](https://www.columbiacc.com/markets/portfolio/commonwealth-hotel-boston/) · [Sunrise Erectors](https://sunriseerectors.com/project/hotel-commonwealth/) · [Mapped enclosed bridge](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/822618764)
- BU calls its former Myles Standish Hall **610 Beacon Street**. The project filing and BU floor plans document the nine-storey residence. The model preserves the rounded point and light wells of the mapped outline. The renovation retained the historic brick-and-stone character and a white roof. [Project filing with plans](https://www.bostonplans.org/getattachment/4b718b69-b0c3-409b-b119-efdca567ca63) · [BU housing floor plans](https://www.bu.edu/housing/residence/large-traditional-style-residences/) · [BU roof and renovation record](https://www.bu.edu/sustainability/projects/610-beacon-street/) · [MDS exterior gallery](https://www.mds-bos.com/boston-university-610-beacon-street-renovation) · [BU naming history](https://www.bu.edu/articles/2024/bu-removes-myles-standish-dorm-name/)
- Joining the survey's fragmented party-wall ways restores the footprint of **491 Commonwealth Avenue**, which OSM identifies as a synagogue. Chabad's directions confirm the address. The Pluralism Project's street photograph records the brick walls over a pale base, entry steps, dark glass door, sign and metal menorah. [Mapped footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/9712516) · [Chabad House](https://www.chabadboston.org/templates/articlecco_cdo/aid/829228/jewish/Directions.htm) · [Pluralism Project photograph](https://pluralism.org/chabad-greater-boston)

## Weather and occasions

- NOAA's 1991–2020 normals at Boston Logan (USW00014739) give average daily highs of 56.4°F in April, 82.1°F in July and 62.1°F in October. The snowfall table gives 14.3 inches for January. Drizzle and umbrellas, green summer crowns and café tables, fall leaves, and bare branches and snow are illustrative seasonal conditions. [NOAA monthly normals, temperature and snowfall tables](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&endDate=9996-12-31&format=pdf&startDate=0001-01-01&stations=USW00014739)
- The Red Sox direct B, C and D riders to Kenmore, then west along Commonwealth and left onto Brookline Avenue to reach Fenway Park across the bridge. The fans in the model follow the first leg of that walk. Their clothes, pennants, numbers and timing are illustrative. [Boston Red Sox walking directions](https://www.mlb.com/redsox/ballpark/transportation/subway)
- The B.A.A. identifies the CITGO sign as the one-mile-to-go landmark and locates a cheer zone at mile 25.2. The race line is the clipped section of OSM relation 11680552 on eastbound way 822618756. On race day the scene reserves the carriageway for runners and removes crossing traffic. Crowd rails, small cheer banners, spectators, runner spacing and speeds are illustrative. [Boston Athletic Association](https://www.baa.org/partner/sponsor/citgo/) · [B.A.A. course map](https://www.baa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Race-Course.pdf) · [Mapped marathon](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/11680552)
- The commission describes the old sign's LEDs and dusk-to-midnight illumination. The evening scene shows a single bright phase of the historic sign with lit windows and lamps. The illumination sequence, exact light levels and window occupancy are illustrative. [Boston Landmarks Commission, section 2.2](https://www.boston.gov/sites/default/files/imce-uploads/2018-09/citgo_sign_subcommittee_final_draft.pdf)
- The source station photographs show deciduous trees on the retail sidewalks. The 17 additional sidewalk trees interpolate this streetscape around the 24 prepared tree positions. Tree sizes and species mix, lamp proportions, pavement modules, architectural trim, unsurveyed roofs and heights, vehicle frequency, trips and café furniture are illustrative. Boston-licensed taxis use white bodies. [Station architect's street views](https://www.dimellashaffer.com/blog/seeing-is-believing/) · [Boston Police, Rule 403](https://police.boston.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Rule-403.pdf)

## Reproduction and checks

Run these commands from this work folder. The source extract stays intact. The cleanup joins 127 complete relation rings along matching endpoints. It can discard a chain that fails to close. For this extract, every assembled chain closed. Eight off-plate waterway tags are omitted from the copy because the boat sampler otherwise tries to construct a hull from the empty water intersection's NaN bounds. Ten duplicated imported right-of-way widths on divided roads are replaced by the kit's mapped-lane calculation. The original 21.6 m width on the one-way segment at the north stair would put the stair in the road.

```sh
pocket_python="$HOME/.venvs/pocket-city/bin/python"
pocket_scripts="$HOME/.codex/skills/pocket-city/scripts"
export PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1
"$pocket_python" clean_osm.py
"$pocket_python" "$pocket_scripts/plot.py" --osm osm-clean.json --lat 42.3489922 --lon -71.0949241 --rotate 6.17 --slide 0 --keep 'Hotel Commonwealth' --out plot.json
"$pocket_python" "$pocket_scripts/prep.py" --osm osm-clean.json --plot plot.json --drive right --out scene.json
"$pocket_python" "$pocket_scripts/build.py" --scene scene.json --city city.json --landmarks landmarks.js --out ../../../../frontend/public/pocket/kenmore.html
node "$pocket_scripts/render.mjs" ../../../../frontend/public/pocket/kenmore.html --out ../shots --season spring,summer,fall,winter,gameday,marathon,night --view iso,bird
```
