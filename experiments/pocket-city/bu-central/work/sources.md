## Seasons and occasions

- **April showers, summer and snow day.** Logan’s 1991–2020 normals give 3.63 inches of April precipitation, a July mean daily maximum of 82.1°F, and 14.3 inches of January snowfall. These inform the wet paving and umbrellas, full green canopy, and snowy bare-tree scenes. [NOAA monthly normals for Boston Logan](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&endDate=9996-12-31&format=pdf&startDate=0001-01-01&stations=USW00014739)
- **Fall color.** Boston’s Arnold Arboretum maple collection provides the regional foliage reference. The maple colors and branching patterns on this block are illustrative. [Arnold Arboretum](https://arboretum.harvard.edu/plants/maple-collection-directions-and-accessibility-information/)
- **Step on the Seal.** Cydney Scott’s photograph shows graduates wearing red gowns and black mortarboards on the Marsh Plaza crest. The drawing uses eleven anonymous graduates and six companions in a new arrangement. [BU Today, May 2022](https://www.bu.edu/today/close-ups/2022/step-on-the-seal/)
- **GSU Farmers Market.** BU Dining places the seasonal market on the George Sherman Union Plaza. The fall scene uses six canopies, produce crates and eighteen visitors. Their number, colors and layout are illustrative. [BU Dining Services](https://www.bu.edu/dining/local-food/)

## Preparation and checks

`clean_osm.py` rejoins endpoints of split multipolygon ways in a separate `osm-clean.json`. It recovered 55 complete rings and dropped zero chains. On 24 one-way Commonwealth Avenue and Massachusetts Turnpike segments, it removed a repeated full-corridor width so the kit could use the mapped carriageway lanes. Both kit pipeline stages read the cleaned copy. Four generated walking routes along the rails and inferred bicycle routes on expressways were removed from the drawing. The street crossings and outer avenue sidewalks remain.

The kit prepared 17 building features, including three without a mapped height or storey count. The field notes distinguish measured footprints and the documented tower height from estimated heights, façade bays, stained glass, seal and dove details, seasonal weather, tree species, riders, traffic, trolley timings, parked vehicles and the summer boat.

The plot check found all three named buildings inside and no famous building cut. The build emitted no warnings. Headless Chrome could not start in the sandbox. `check-frames.cjs` reads the worker from the finished HTML on disk and produces 33 inspection images covering six orbit angles, bird’s-eye and isometric views, all seasons and occasions, and landmark close-ups. It also checks 20 stationary and moving-camera frames on desktop and phone worker profiles, event object counts, trolley dwells and departures, and the boat’s position in water. Browser controls and live frame rate were outside the renderer check.
